# Code of conduct

Orbit adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to all project spaces, including
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where people represent the project.

## Summary

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone. We
expect participants to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including toward differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal or political attacks, sexualized language
or imagery, publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately
through their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review each
report and take whatever action they judge appropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of
the Contributor Covenant.
